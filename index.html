What we build

Heed is developing an AI health and nutrition platform. It brings together what a person eats, data from their wearables and smart scale, and their blood test results, and gives guidance calibrated to the goal they choose: weight, longevity, focus or performance.

Alongside the app, Heed is planning a curated wellness store and partner blood testing.

The platform offers lifestyle guidance and educational context. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment.

Where we are

The app is in build and is not yet available. Heed is based in London.
