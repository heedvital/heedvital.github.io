What this website collects

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It has no forms and no accounts.

The site is hosted by GitHub. When a page is served, GitHub's systems record technical information such as the visitor's IP address, to keep the service secure and working. Heed does not access or use this information. GitHub's own privacy statement explains how it handles it.

When you email us

If you email Heed, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use this only to read and reply to your message and to keep a record of our correspondence.

The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. We keep correspondence for up to two years after the conversation ends, unless we need it longer for a legal reason. Email is provided by Namecheap, which processes messages on our behalf.

Please do not send health information by email. The website is not the place for it.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone except the service providers named above. Some of these providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your data
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our using it
  • ask for your data in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email us at the address above. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes
